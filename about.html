About the practice

Therapy rooted in clarity, care, and collaboration.

Esfahani Therapy is a boutique psychotherapy practice that centers depth work, practical tools, and a grounded relationship. Sessions are designed to help you feel more steady, connected, and self-trusting in the life you are building.

Schedule a call Read reflections

Guiding values

We approach every client with warmth, transparency, and respect for the complexity of their story.

Relational depth

The therapeutic relationship is a living space to explore patterns, repair ruptures, and experience genuine connection.

Practical integration

Insight matters, and so does action. We translate insight into everyday practices that create steadiness and agency.

Trauma-informed care

Sessions are paced thoughtfully, honoring your nervous system, cultural context, and readiness for change.

Credentials & training

Our clinician draws from integrative, evidence-based modalities while staying attuned to each client’s lived experience.

Licensed clinical therapist
Advanced training in psychodynamic therapy
Ongoing consultation in relational and trauma-informed practice
Specialized focus on anxiety, identity, and life transitions

What to expect

The first few sessions focus on getting to know you—your history, goals, and the patterns you want to shift. From there we build a cadence that supports meaningful progress, whether you are seeking clarity, healing, or growth.

Virtual appointments are available for clients across California. In-person sessions can be arranged by request.
